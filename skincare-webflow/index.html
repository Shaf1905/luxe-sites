Ingredient explorer

What the forest
knows.

Sphagnum palustre

Peat moss holds up to twenty times its dry weight in water. Its extract draws moisture into the skin and keeps it there, which is why Moss Water feels like a drink rather than a coat.

20×its weight in water
Cetraria islandica

A lichen that has soothed sore throats in Iceland for centuries. On skin, its polysaccharides calm itch and redness after wind, cold or a bad reaction.

400years of folk use
Vaccinium vitis-idaea

Cold-pressed from the tiny seeds of wild lingonberries. Rich in omega-3 and vitamin E, it restores the oils that harsh winters and harsh cleansers strip away.

Ω3and vitamin E
Myrica gale

Sweet gale grows in Nordic bogs and was once brewed into beer. Its oil is gently antibacterial and smells green and resinous, so we never add fragrance.
