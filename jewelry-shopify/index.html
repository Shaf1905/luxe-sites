How it's made

From scrap
to heirloom.

01

Melt

We buy back old jewelry and refine gold from discarded electronics. It's melted at 1,064°C until it's pure again.

02

Cast

The liquid metal is poured into moulds carved by hand in wax. Each cast is unique, down to the tiny surface marks.

03

Polish

Four rounds of filing and polishing take each piece from rough to mirror. It takes about six hours per ring.
