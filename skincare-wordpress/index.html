    Ingredient glossary

    Every word on
    the label, explained.

    No marketing names. Here's what each ingredient is, why it's there, and how much we use.

    Glycerin

    Humectant

    A plant-derived sugar alcohol that pulls water into the top layer of skin. Unglamorous, cheap and one of the best-studied moisturisers there is.

    In: Clear Cleanse 5%, Daily Moisture 7%

    Panthenol

    Soother

    Pro-vitamin B5. It calms irritation and helps skin repair after shaving, sun or over-exfoliating.

    In: Barrier Mist 2%, Daily Moisture 1%

    Azelaic acid

    Clarifier

    Gently clears pores and fades marks left by breakouts. Kinder than most acids, so it suits sensitive skin.

    In: Clarity Serum 10%

    Ceramide NP

    Barrier

    One of the lipids your skin's own barrier is made of. Topping it up keeps water in and irritants out.

    In: Daily Moisture, Rich Balm

    Squalane

    Emollient

    A light, stable oil that mirrors the one your skin makes. Softens without clogging, even on oily skin.

    In: Night Oil 100%
