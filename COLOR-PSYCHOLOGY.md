# Color psychology applied to the 9 sites

Each niche gets the emotions its buyers shop on, and each site gets one palette that expresses them. Product tints inside the cards were left alone, because they describe each product (a green bottle for a herb scent, and so on).

## Perfume: sensual, mysterious, romantic
People buy scent on mood and desire. Deep plum and wine suggest mystery and sensuality, gold adds prestige, and blush suggests romance.

| Site | Palette | Background | Text | Accent | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maison Vey (Shopify) | Aubergine & champagne | `#120A12` | `#F3E9EE` | `#D9B77C` | Plum-black reads as mystery and luxury; champagne gold signals a premium house. |
| Flore Noire (Webflow) | Crimson rose & plum-black | `#07040A` | `#F1E8EA` | `#C4183C` | Red is the color of passion and desire. Crimson feels more sensual than the previous orange-red. Used on large type only (3.4:1 passes for large text). |
| Petal & Pyre (WordPress) | Blush & mauve | `#F7EDEE` | `#33202B` | `#9E4A6E` | Blush and dusty rose suggest tenderness and romance, which suits a garden-floral brand. |

## Skincare: trust, calm, purity, healing
People buy skincare on trust and safety. Soft blues calm and build trust, greens suggest nature and healing, and clean whites suggest purity.

| Site | Palette | Background | Text | Accent | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Halcyon (Shopify) | Aqua & white | `#EDF5F4` | `#12282A` | `#1F7A78` | Aqua sits between blue (trust, clinical) and green (healing), which suits reactive-skin care. |
| Mosse (Webflow) | Forest & fresh sage | `#0E1A14` | `#EAF1E6` | `#B7D3A0` | A greener background and a fresher, lighter sage say renewal rather than heavy olive. |
| Sable (WordPress) | Clean linen & eucalyptus | `#F4F0EA` | `#1D2322` | `#3F7366` | Near-white linen says purity and simplicity; eucalyptus green is calm and clean. Deepened to pass AA (4.8:1). |

## Jewelry: prestige, value, romance
People buy jewelry on status, trust in the value, and emotion. Black with gold says wealth, ivory with champagne says bridal elegance, and navy with sapphire says trust and loyalty.

| Site | Palette | Background | Text | Accent | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Molten (Shopify) | Onyx & metallic gold | `#0A0806` | `#F2EBDD` | `#D4AF37` | The classic luxury pairing. A warmer onyx and true metallic gold read as wealth and permanence. |
| Aveline (Webflow) | Ivory & champagne | `#F8F4EE` | `#2A2220` | `#8A673B` | Ivory says purity and romance; champagne gold is elegant without shouting. Espresso text is softer than pure black. Deepened to pass AA (4.7:1). |
| Celeste (WordPress) | Midnight navy & sapphire | `#050A1F` | `#EAF0FF` | `#6E95FF` | Blue builds trust and loyalty, which fits engraved gifts. The richer sapphire matches the stones. |

## Contrast
All body text is above 13:1. Every accent passes WCAG AA for the size it's used at.
